Shopping trip

Build a fixed checklist from the meal plan after pantry subtraction. Checking purchased items automatically adds their actual quantity to your pantry.

AnyList export remains available as a backup. Checked items remain on the current trip; they can be unchecked only while their pantry lot remains unchanged. Private Google Drive synchronization between devices is still pending.

Use Cook beside a planned recipe to confirm the actual ingredients used. Pantry deductions and cooking history update together.

Cooking history

Select Cook beside a planned recipe and adjust the ingredients you actually used. Confirming updates the pantry. You can optionally record the finished batch weight for MacroFactor.
