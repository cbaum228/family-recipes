Shopping trip

Build a fixed checklist from the meal plan after pantry subtraction. Checking purchased items automatically adds their actual quantity to your pantry.

AnyList options: Copy names for AnyList pastes directly into your existing shared grocery list, with quantities shown in parentheses as part of the name. To get separate quantity fields, choose Copy recipe ingredients (Metric) for g/ml or Copy recipe ingredients (US) for lb/oz/fl oz. Create a temporary recipe in AnyList, paste into its Ingredients section, then select Add all ingredients to list and choose your existing shared list. Both export buttons use the same missing-ingredient quantities; only the displayed units differ. Review converted amounts before shopping. Avoid importing both formats for the same trip to prevent duplicates.

Checked items remain on the current trip; they can be unchecked only while their pantry lot remains unchanged. Shared cross-device shopping requires the private Firebase household connection; it remains local-only until configured.

Use Cook beside a planned recipe to confirm the actual ingredients used. Pantry deductions and cooking history update together.

Cooking history

Select Cook beside a planned recipe and adjust the ingredients you actually used. Confirming updates the pantry. You can optionally record the finished batch weight for MacroFactor.
