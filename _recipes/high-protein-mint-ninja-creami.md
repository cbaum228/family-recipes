## Reconstruction and verification notes

This **was made April 2026**. Actual mixture: **11.5 oz Member's Mark vanilla ultrafiltered milk (30 g protein labeled)**, **5.3 oz Oikos Triple Zero vanilla**, **½ scoop Jocko Molk vanilla**, **¼ tsp peppermint extract**, and salt. It spun very creamy. The pinch of salt noticeably improved flavor, but the peppermint flavor was too menthol-heavy. You prefer **spearmint** for future batches.

Dark chocolate chips and Thin Mint cookie mix-ins were actually tried; quantities weren't saved, and some incompletely distributed frozen bits were surprisingly bitter. Baseline excludes those optional mix-ins; a future spearmint version is not claimed as cooked. Actual protein-powder scoop weight, total nutritional macros and final pint weight require verification.
