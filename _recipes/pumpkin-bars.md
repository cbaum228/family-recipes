## Original US measurements

- 4 eggs
- ¾ cup melted coconut oil
- ¾ cup sugar
- 15 oz plain pumpkin
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- ½ tsp ground cloves
- ½ tsp ground nutmeg
